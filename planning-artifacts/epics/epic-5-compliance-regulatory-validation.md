# Epic 5: Compliance & Regulatory Validation

Users can automatically validate their drafted proposals against regulatory frameworks to ensure full compliance.
**FRs covered:** FR10

## Story 5.1: Automated Compliance Check

As a Bid Manager,
I want the system to automatically validate my proposal against the ZOP framework,
So that I don't miss any legal requirements.

**Acceptance Criteria:**

**Given** a completed draft
**When** I run the compliance check
**Then** the Compliance Metric Card shows fractional compliance
**And** expands to detail any missing mandatory items
