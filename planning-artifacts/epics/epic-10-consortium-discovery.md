# Epic 10: Consortium & Partner Discovery

This epic provides tools to help users find and assemble consortium partners for large grant applications, including an AI-powered partner suggestion engine.

**FRs covered:** None from the initial list, this is a future-facing epic based on the UX supplement.

## Stories

### Story 10.1: Search for Consortium Partners
As a user preparing a large grant application,
I want to search a database of organizations to find potential consortium partners based on their expertise, country, and past EU project experience,
So that I can build a strong and compliant consortium.

**Acceptance Criteria:**
**Given** I am in the "Consortium Finder" for a grant
**When** I enter search criteria (e.g., "SME in Germany with AI expertise")
**Then** I see a list of matching organizations with profiles detailing their experience.

### Story 10.2: AI-Powered Partner Suggestion
As a user building a consortium,
I want the AI to suggest potential partners that would complement my team's strengths and fill any gaps,
So that I can discover the best possible partners for my grant application.

**Acceptance Criteria:**
**Given** I have defined the scope of my grant and my own organization's role
**When** I click "Suggest Partners"
**Then** the AI provides a ranked list of recommended partners with an explanation of why each is a good fit.
