# Epic 9: Post-Award Management & Reporting

This epic extends the platform's value beyond just winning bids, providing tools to manage awarded grants, track budgets, and generate periodic reports.

**FRs covered:** None from the initial list, this is a future-facing epic based on the UX supplement.

## Stories

### Story 9.1: Manage Awarded Grants
As a grant manager,
I want to track my awarded grants in a dedicated section of the platform,
So that I can manage their lifecycle, deadlines, and deliverables post-award.

**Acceptance Criteria:**
**Given** I have won a grant
**When** I mark the opportunity as "Awarded"
**Then** it appears in my "Active Grants" dashboard where I can manage its details.

### Story 9.2: Track Budget Expenditure
As a grant manager,
I want to track budget expenditure against the grant's cost categories,
So that I can ensure financial compliance and monitor the burn rate.

**Acceptance Criteria:**
**Given** I am viewing an active grant
**When** I navigate to the "Budget" tab
**Then** I can log expenses against predefined categories and see a real-time summary of spending vs. budget.

### Story 9.3: Generate Periodic Reports
As a grant manager,
I want the system to help me generate the periodic technical and financial reports required by the funding agency,
So that I can simplify the reporting process and reduce administrative overhead.

**Acceptance Criteria:**
**Given** a reporting period is ending for an active grant
**When** I click "Generate Report"
**Then** the system creates a report template pre-filled with data from the platform, such as WP progress and financial statements.
